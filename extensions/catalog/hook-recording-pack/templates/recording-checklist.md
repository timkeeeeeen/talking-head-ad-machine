# Recording checklist

- Quiet room and clear microphone placement.
- Lens near eye level with clean headroom.
- Enough horizontal and vertical space for the target crop.
- Stable exposure and focus.
- Complete thoughts rather than fragments.
- Two seconds of quiet before and after each take.
- Three alternate hooks with the same body.
- One clear CTA take.
- Product screens or demonstrations captured separately.
- Claims checked against evidence before recording.
